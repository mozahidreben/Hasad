About Hasad

A harvest of quality, raised right

Hasad ("harvest" in Arabic) began with a simple belief: that great poultry starts long before it reaches a plate — with healthy breeding stock, disciplined biosecurity, and people who treat every flock like it's the only one. Today we operate an integrated network of farms, hatcheries and processing facilities across the Kingdom, supplying fresh chicken, poultry products, day-old chicks and feed to retailers, restaurants and growers who expect nothing less than consistent, verifiable quality.
